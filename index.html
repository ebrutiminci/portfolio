When the bank goes quiet

On 20 October 2026 a Google Cloud failure took Revolut down for most of a working day. I've been a customer since 2022, and my card failed at a supermarket till over a €2 lunch. The outage wasn't Revolut's fault. The silence inside the app was a design choice, so I redesigned it.

19 of 22public accounts from that day describe a card failing at a till. The feeling people named most was embarrassment.
  1. P0The tillDeclined, no reason given
  2. P0The ATMFailed, no notification
  3. P0The app"Something went wrong"
  4. P1SupportA chat that was down too
  5. P1The explanationAn email, hours later

The proposal is an in-app outage mode: an honest banner, the last known balance, a push at the moment of decline, an honest support screen and a personal recovery summary, with the metrics to track it.

UX Research App

A tool that helps founders turn an idea into hypotheses, interview guides and surveys. While you write a question, it flags the ones that will get you polite answers instead of true ones. It follows my own research rule: ask about the past, never "would you use…".

Example

Would you use an app that reminds you to drink water?

Future question · yes/no question

Tell me about the last time you forgot to drink water during a workday.

I wrote the product spec, designed the flow and built it in plain JavaScript, deployed on Vercel. I used it to run a survey study and rank the insights.

Feeling at Home in the City

How do people feel in Ljubljana's streets, and does it change with who they are? For my master's thesis I designed an experience-sampling app: two short surveys, then daily check-ins, with an illustrated city that grows as people take part. 31 people took part, sending 98 check-ins tagged with where they were, which I mapped in QGIS.

General notes

From buildings to products

Architecture taught me to design for people who live with my decisions every day, and to care about grids, scale and typography. A psychology minor taught me to ask why people behave the way they do. I bring both to digital products, and I prototype in code so I can test ideas with real people sooner.
